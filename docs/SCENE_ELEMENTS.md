# Marble Madness 3D Scenery Elements

The prototype uses the procedural models below. Practice Race placements
and recorded heights are specific to this course.
Meshes are expressed in BabylonJS coordinates, with Y up.
One horizontal unit corresponds to one course tile.

| Element                        | Construction and Scene Identifier                                                                                                                                                                                        | Placement and Variants                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Stone pyramid                  | [`createPracticeTower`](../src/rendering/createPracticeTower.ts), `practice-point-0` and `practice-point-1`                                                                                                              | Place origin at landing level, below the tip. Base spans −1 to +2 tiles on X and Z. Shoulder is 1.08 units wide and about 0.61 units above the landing. Cap rises 2 units higher. Each lower face bears three stones, with a less tapered center stone. Provide one stone material per palette. |
| Small pyramid                  | [`createPracticeSpike`](../src/rendering/createPracticeTower.ts), `practice-channel-spike-0` and `practice-channel-spike-1`                                                                                              | Place origin on the flat ledge. Four faces and a material shared with the other pyramids.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Straight or angled guardrail   | [`createPracticeGuardrail`](../src/rendering/createPracticeGuardrail.ts), groups `practice-rail-0` to `practice-rail-6`                                                                                                  | Provide base and top of each post in order. Rails connect tops, including on slopes. Provide the desired painted material.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Paving, steps, pits, and walls | [`terrainMesh`](../src/game/modern/terrainMesh.ts), [`terrainCliffs`](../src/game/modern/terrainCliffs.ts), `terrain-surface` and `terrain-cliffs`                                                                       | Start from the course recording and keep openings. Palette and cutouts in `stylePracticeWalls` are tuned to Practice Race.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Guide arrows                   | [`createPracticeMarkings`](../src/rendering/createPracticeMarkings.ts), `practice-arrow-x`, `practice-arrow-z`, and `practice-arrow-center`                                                                              | Paint pattern is generated separately. Mesh follows ramp triangles, whose coordinates are specific to Practice Race.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Marble                         | [`createLivePracticeView`](../src/rendering/createLivePracticeView.ts) and [`createTerrainStudy`](../src/rendering/createTerrainStudy.ts), `practice-player`                                                             | Motion comes from the arcade pose. Marble rolls based on successive received positions.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 3–4–5–6 markings and GOAL sign | [`createPracticeNumberSigns`](../src/rendering/createPracticeNumberSigns.ts) and [`createPracticeGoal`](../src/rendering/createPracticeGoal.ts), `practice-number-sign-0` to `practice-number-sign-2`, `practice-finish` | Markings follow tiles. Banner switches material upon receiving the finish state.                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Finish flags                   | [`createPracticeGoalFlags`](../src/rendering/createPracticeGoal.ts), `practice-goal-flag-0` and `practice-goal-flag-1`                                                                                                   | Two poles at the ramp edges. Cloth is animated by the render loop.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

Pyramid anchors, the seven guardrail paths, and tile support calculation
are in [`createPracticeLandmarks`](../src/rendering/createPracticeLandmarks.ts).
To add a course, record its landings and anchor points, then call the same
constructors with course-specific names. To change colors, share one material
across objects of the same palette. The `point` material from
[`createPracticeSurfaceMaterial`](../src/rendering/createPracticeSurfaceMaterial.ts)
preserves stone joints and grain when `diffuseColor` is changed.
The scene owns and disposes meshes and materials. The guardrail node groups
posts and rails so they can be moved together.
